What it monitors.

01 — Detection

Bead deviation

Angle measurement on every bead, every layer. Deviations classified by severity and logged with frame-accurate timestamps.

02 — Quality

Surface quality

Consistency and uniformity checks across each pass. Irregular surfaces flagged before they compound into structural issues.

03 — Structure

Layer integrity

Bonding and continuity analysis between layers. Gaps, voids, and adhesion anomalies detected and recorded in the event log.

Post-Processing Analysis

Inspect every bead.
Before it becomes a problem.

01
Upload a bead photograph

Take a photo of any printed bead — phone, site camera, or inspection rig. Upload directly through the Post-Processing interface. No special hardware required.

02
AI scores the quality

Our vision system analyses the image for surface consistency, geometry, voids, and bonding anomalies. Each assessment returns a numerical quality score alongside annotated findings.

03
Defects flagged by category

Surface cracks, air pockets, delamination, and geometric deviation are classified separately — so you know exactly what type of issue you're looking at, not just that something is wrong.

04
Document and sign off

Every result is saved with the original image and timestamp. Your inspection log builds automatically — giving you an auditable record of every bead assessment across the full project.
