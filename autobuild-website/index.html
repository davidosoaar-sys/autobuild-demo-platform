The intelligence layer your 3DCP project is missing.

Robotic concrete printing can cut labour costs, eliminate formwork, and compress build programmes. But the results depend entirely on how well the process is managed. Right now, most teams are doing that manually.

AutoBuild is the software that sits between your design file and your printer. It plans the path, checks your conditions, and watches every layer so your team can focus on building, not firefighting.

The difference between a good print and a costly one usually comes down to planning and real-time visibility. Not the printer itself.

AutoBuild Slicer

From model to print path in minutes.

Upload your model, set your conditions, and AutoBuild generates an optimised print path. Your concrete mix, weather data, and build timing are all factored in automatically.

Step 01

Upload your model

Drop in your STL file. AutoBuild reads the geometry, detects units, and prepares it for slicing with no manual setup required.

Step 02

Set your conditions

Choose your mix, enter your location, and set your start time. AutoBuild pulls live weather and calculates your usable print window.

Step 03

Export and print

Get your optimised G-code with risk flags and a full layer visualisation. Everything reviewed before the printer moves.

AutoBuild Monitor

Every layer, watched.

AutoBuild connects to your site camera and analyses every layer as it's printed. Problems are flagged as they happen, not after the concrete sets.

  • Frame-by-frame deviation tracking. Measures bead placement against your intended path on every layer.
  • Instant alerts. Your team is notified the moment something drifts, not at the end of the shift.
  • Automated quality logs. Timestamped records from every session, ready for sign-off and compliance.
